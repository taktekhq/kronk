# TDA7266 Dual Channel Audio Amplifier

7W + 7W amplifier module with onboard volume potentiometer and screw terminals for speakers.

## Specs

- Chip: TDA7266
- Channels: 2 (left and right)
- Output: 7W + 7W
- Speaker impedance: 4 to 8 ohm, recommended 5 to 15W
- Supply: 3 to 18V DC
- PCB: 45mm x 33mm
- Terminals: 2-way, 5.08mm pitch
