# UGreen 2 in 1 SD/TF Card Reader (CM304 80191)

Compact card reader with both USB-C and USB-A plugs. Used to flash SD cards.

## Specs

- Brand: UGREEN, model CM304
- Input: 1x USB-A, 1x USB-C
- Slots: 1x SD, 1x TF (micro SD)
- USB 3.0, up to 5Gbps
- Transfer speed: up to 170MB/s
- Cards: SD 3.0, micro SD
- Max capacity: 512GB measured, 2TB theoretical
- Works on Windows, macOS, Linux
- Color: black
