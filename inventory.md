# Purchase Inventory

Record of all equipment purchased for the kronk video intercom project.

**Project context:** A DIY video intercom for the home building gate. When someone rings the doorbell at the gate, the goal is to see who it is (video), talk with them (two-way audio), and open the gate remotely (buzzer via relay). Currently in the testing phase for one household, with other tenants to be added later.
